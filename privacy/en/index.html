Flick – Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · Effective October 6, 2026

In short: Flick processes your photos on your device only. We run no servers that receive photos, metadata, voice recordings or other personal data, we use no analytics or advertising tools, and we sell no data. This document sets out the details.

1. General and who we are

This document describes how the Flick app (the "App") handles information, in line with the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981, as amended (including Amendment 13), and its regulations (the "Law"). The App is operated by Ofek Moshe Moshkovitz, exempt dealer, Karmiel, Israel (the "Operator", "we").

Use of the App is also subject to Apple's standard license agreement and to Flick's Terms of Use.

2. The core principle: processing on your device

  • The App does not send photos, videos, photo data (metadata), audio recordings or voice transcripts to the Operator or to any third party acting on its behalf.
  • All analysis, including detecting similar or duplicate photos, recognizing screenshots and receipts, and calculating storage use, runs on your device using iOS system components.
  • The Operator has no user accounts, no user database, and no access to your device or its contents.

Accordingly, the information described in section 3 is stored only on your device, under your control, and is not held by us.

4. Permissions on your device

  • Access to your photo library: necessary for the App to work and for deleting what you choose. Deletion happens only after confirmation in the iOS prompt, and deleted photos move to "Recently Deleted" according to system settings. If you use iCloud Photos, a deletion may also update your other Apple devices.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only if you explicitly turn on voice-controlled cleaning. Recognition runs on the device only and the audio is not sent to a server. If on-device recognition isn't supported for your language or device, the feature won't run.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a daily "On This Day" reminder that is created and scheduled locally on the device.

You can change or revoke any permission at any time in iOS Settings. Revoking photo access prevents the App from working.

5. Apple services and third parties

The App uses Apple services only, and these are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not the Operator's:

  • App Store and StoreKit: purchasing a subscription or one-time purchase, trial period, restoring purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store a payment method, and entitlement is verified with Apple on the device.
  • Sign in with Apple: optional, used for a display name only.
  • iCloud Photos: if a photo is stored in the cloud and isn't available on the device, iOS may download it from iCloud in order to display it. That download is between your device and Apple only.
  • Shared cleaning (SharePlay): if you choose to use it in a FaceTime call, delete/keep decisions and internal photo identifiers (not the photos themselves) are sent to the other participants through Apple's services.
  • Crash reports and usage data: if you enabled sharing with developers in iOS settings, Apple may give us aggregated, non-identifying data. We do not link it to you.
  • Legal documents inside the App: when this policy or the Terms of Use open inside the App, the page is loaded from the Operator's hosting service (GitHub Pages) like any website, and the host may log an IP address. We receive no data from the App in this context.

The App contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) for analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). Accordingly, the App does not request tracking permission (ATT).

6. Transfers outside Israel

The Operator does not transfer personal data outside Israel. Processing by Apple, as described in section 5, is carried out by Apple as an independent party under its own privacy policy and is not a transfer made on our behalf.

The flick-legal website: if you open this policy or the Terms of Use in a browser, the site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which may log your IP address. Inside the App these documents load from the device and make no network request.

7. Information security

Not collecting data on servers is our main security measure. Local data is protected by iOS sandboxing and encryption, and at the level your device is protected (passcode, system updates). We recommend locking your device and keeping the operating system up to date. Every information system is exposed to risk, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Retention and deletion

The Operator keeps no information about you. Local data stays on your device until you remove the App, at which point it is deleted. Some data (for example streaks and statistics) can be reset by removing and reinstalling. Invoices and payment records are kept by Apple alone, in accordance with its obligations.

9. Your rights

Under the Law, you are entitled to inspect information about you held in a database and to request its correction or deletion. Because the Operator holds no information about you, there is nothing for us to provide, correct or delete. You control local data directly: by changing settings, revoking permissions, or removing the App. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us at the email in section 14 and we will reply within 30 days.

Requests concerning your Apple ID, purchases or iCloud should be directed to Apple.

10. Cancelling a subscription and refunds

Managing a subscription, cancellation and refunds are described in the Terms of Use (Subscriptions and purchases). Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com and do not affect your rights under the Israeli Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981.

11. Minors

The App is intended for people aged 13 and over. A minor under 18 may use it with the consent of a parent or guardian, and purchases by minors are subject to Apple's parental controls (for example Ask to Buy). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and we hold no such information on servers.

12. Database

To the best of our understanding, and because the information is not held by us, we are not required to register a database or appoint a privacy protection officer. If that changes, we will update this policy and take the required steps.

13. Users outside Israel

The App is available in several languages and countries. Because the Operator neither collects nor holds personal data about users, there is no information to access, correct, port or delete, including under data protection laws that apply where you live (such as the GDPR in the European Union). If you believe information about you is held, contact us and we will respond in accordance with the applicable law. Your right to complain to your local supervisory authority is preserved.

14. Contact

For questions, requests or inquiries about privacy: sales@plmos.dev. You may also contact the Privacy Protection Authority at the Israeli Ministry of Justice.
